Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: March 20, 2024

How to Delete Your Data

The Facebook Verification Agent does not store any personal data beyond the immediate processing needs. All data is automatically deleted after the verification process is complete.

To Request Data Deletion:

  1. Send an email to eric@workflowchampions.com
  2. Include "Data Deletion Request" in the subject line
  3. Provide the following information:
    • Full name used in the verification request
    • Date of verification request (if known)
    • Property address used in the verification

We will process your request within 7 business days and send you a confirmation email once completed.
